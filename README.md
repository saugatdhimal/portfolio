## 👋 My Story

Hi, I’m **Saugat Dhimal** — a developer shaped more by questions than by instructions.

My journey into software development didn’t begin with a perfect roadmap. It started with curiosity — breaking things, rebuilding them, and slowly understanding how the digital world fits together behind the scenes.

What began as simple experiments turned into a craft I genuinely care about.

Over the past couple of years, I’ve grown into a developer who values **clarity over complexity**, **systems over hacks**, and **impact over noise**.

I don’t just write code to make things work — I build to make things *make sense*.
